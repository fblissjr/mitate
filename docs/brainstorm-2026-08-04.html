1. The problem and the opportunity, in one paragraph

The tooling has outrun the films. No new example film has ever shipped from this repo: every file in plugin/skills/mitate/examples/ arrived in the single migration commit on 2026-07-24 (git log --diff-filter=A -- plugin/skills/mitate/examples/ shows one commit). Meanwhile the defect record runs about 4 product bugs to 25 tooling bugs (docs/representation.md, "Where defects actually come from"). At the same time, REP1 through REP3 landed on three consecutive days (plan.md stamps: 0.17.0, 0.18.0, 0.19.0), so the checking tools are the strongest they have ever been, and a film is now the cheapest it has ever been to make correctly. The opportunity: the next film is four things at once. It is product evidence. It is the first end-to-end exercise of the REP work. It feeds the pattern ledger, whose REP5 gate cannot fire without films shipping. And if a fresh session builds it from the docs alone, it is also the deferred VISION test ("a session arriving with no context can find what it needs and act correctly"). The risk of waiting: VISION's flywheel, where each film improves the engine for the next one, has had nothing to grind for eleven days.

2. The paths, and their tradeoffs

Three live candidates. They are not mutually exclusive; the real question is order.

A. REP4 first (derive extents instead of hand-typing them)

For: this is the biggest recorded source of product bugs. Hand-declared extents were wrong in 3 of 5 worked examples in plan.md, the pattern ledger counts the shape at 6, and breakdown.md calls it "the layer's most expensive gap". The test fixture already exists and is already wired in: bracket-corpus.js row 11 pins the walker's wrong width, green in today's gate. Against: it is one more tooling phase on top of eleven tooling days, and it carries the track's hardest design tension. Deriving extents means measuring geometry. Measuring geometry belongs to probe, and probe's exception to the prime directive lapses if it ever joins "a pipeline that produces an artifact" (CLAUDE.md). A step that writes measured extents into SUBJECTS walks right up to that line. The binding terms are in representation.md, decision point 4. Read those before designing anything.

B. A film first

For: everything in section 1. Either a portfolio film from plan.md's test cases (the-briefing, rube-goldberg) or a small commissioned short. Run it cold, with a fresh session working from the docs alone, and it doubles as the deferred docs-only test (postmortem 2026-08-03, forward item 6). Against: the extent-bug class that REP4 exists to close is still open, so the film will probably hit it. Though hitting it with tools that can measure it (probe) is also exactly the fresh evidence REP4's design wants.

C. Phase 4, starting through R5's remnant

The physics bake is the owner's standing priority on the capability ladder (plan.md: "Phase 4 moves ahead of Phase 3"). The cheap first step is R5 item 4, a kinematic-body amendment to physics-bake-proposal.md. That is just a document edit, and it also moves restructure-2026-07.md toward its self-deletion gate; R5's open items are that amendment plus the deliberately-unpromoted hide/subjectFromObject half. Against: the bake is the project's biggest build, and starting it with zero films shipped on the new tool stack means its acceptance film (rube-goldberg) becomes the first film through everything else at once.

The lean, stated as a lean: B, then A, then C. Do C's document amendment whenever convenient, since it is small and unblocks other things. A film first converts the tooling investment into observed value, gives REP4's design a fresh motivating case, and tests the docs cold. The counterargument, ship the guard before the film that needs it, is real. It is the owner's call, which is why this stays a brainstorm item instead of a working-plan edit.

4. Drift found this span, and where it strikes next

Found and fixed across August 3 and 4 (receipts in the span postmortem and today's log): stale version numbers (plan.md twice, working-plan twice, docs/README once, all still saying "0.18.1" for work that landed as 0.18.2), and comments claiming controls that did not match reality (static.yml said bracket-corpus.js was unbuilt after it shipped; instruments.md's SIZES sentence). Two transferable lessons, both now carried by a control or a procedure: when a version number moves, grep for the number itself across all tracked prose rather than working from a remembered list of surfaces; and .github/workflows/*.yml now sits in audit-claims' blind-spot routing.

Where drift likely strikes next, measured:

  • The provenance-window class has a queue. Reference docs accumulate dated amendments above their "Not here" line until that line slides past selfcheck's 2500-byte header window. instruments.md hit this in the morning (fixed by moving the line up); breakdown.md sat 325 bytes from the boundary in the afternoon (byte-offset scan over every reference, 2026-08-04) and got moved preemptively in 0.19.1. Every other reference sits under 1,700 bytes. The structural fix, putting the "Not here" line right after the founding provenance paragraph with amendments below it, is now the shape of the two files that matter. Adopt it in the others whenever they next gain an amendment. Disposition: a convention, not machinery.
  • Gate cost is creeping. gate.yml's comment says about 6m30s (measured 2026-08-02, dated, so not false). The last four green runs took 7m01s to 7m48s (gh run list timestamps, 2026-08-04), with bracket-corpus.js disclosing about 2 minutes of that at its own site. The repo's own doctrine says a slow gate is one people learn to route around. Proposed disposition: a watch threshold, not work. Revisit the gate's composition when a green run first exceeds 10 minutes.
  • The handoff memo rotted by design, and the owner retired it on 2026-08-04. Its predecessor "rotted four times in one day" (its own header). It failed spine rule 0: gitignored, so unreachable from any clone, and nothing in CLAUDE.md pointed at it, so a session had to be told it existed. And it grew the untracked obligation in section 3. What stays is the tracked path: orientation is docs/README's work-next row plus the day's log. The film field report is a different, living convention: after building a film, the builder writes up what they had to build twice, and those write-ups feed pattern-ledger.md's counts. Older records call it a "film handoff"; it was renamed and given a definition at its home on 2026-08-04, after the owner reasonably asked what it even was. Disposition: done. Memo deleted, unique content graduated, the surviving convention named and defined, memory notes updated.
