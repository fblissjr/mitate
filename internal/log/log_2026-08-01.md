## Findings

`Finding:` **`Function.prototype.toString()` under Bun returns a re-print of the
parsed AST, not source text.** `const p = ctx.page, d = ctx.dur;` comes back as
`const { page: p, dur: d } = ctx;` — a destructuring pattern that was never
typed. Consequence, found the hard way: the first mutant written to prove the
new cross-check red **came back green**, because Bun had normalised the mutation
into the very shape the guard looks for. That mutant was not a mutation. It is
now an arm of `bracket-driver.js` expecting exit 0, so if Bun ever stops
normalising, the arm goes red and says what changed.

`Finding:` **Bun also merges adjacent `const` declarations** into one declarator
list, so `const {...} = ctx;` followed by `const frames = [];` re-prints as
`const {...} = ctx, frames = [];`. A regex anchored on the semicolon
false-redded `checkDeterminism` the moment it was written. The guard was right
that it could not read the pattern and wrong about why — which is the failure
mode worth remembering, because a tripwire firing for the wrong reason reads
exactly like one firing for the right one.

`Finding:` **the C4 defect is worse than the plan said.** `working-plan.md` had
"a check computing against `undefined` will often still emit something
plausible." Measured, by moving the setup assignment after the advisory loop:
one correct scene drew a hard `render is 100.0% near-black` while
`checkFramingInvariance` went **silently all-clear on the same run** — every
window shape sampled at NaN is identical, and a check comparing a frame against
itself cannot fail. Confidently wrong on one arm and quietly powerless on the
other, from one missing key. Both halves of this repo's two favourite failure
modes, from a single `undefined`.

`Finding:` **presence vs definedness was a real fork, not a style choice.** Every
one of the nine corpus scenes exports `window.BEATS`, so `k in ctx` and
`ctx[k] !== undefined` are indistinguishable on the corpus and the wrong one
would have shipped green. Measured against a BEATS-stripped scene: presence
passes with the intended `skipped` warnings; definedness fails a legitimate
scene outright and blames smoke.js for it.

## Where the subagent was wrong, which also matters

It reported that the byte-unchanged clause "did not reproduce", having run the
repo's documented procedure with **unpinned playwright (1.62.1) and no `three`
installed** — two templates failed `[self-contained]` on both sides of its own
comparison. What it actually observed was the `after-hours.html` flake, not a
refutation. Re-run properly: 8 more paired full-corpus runs, all clean.

**But its underlying point survived the correction, and that is the lesson.**

2 of 2 events under full corpus, 0 of 17 isolated. And `[source, webgpu]` had
been offered as evidence for a `WEBGPU=metal` reading it cannot carry: it reports
which three.js backend ran, not which launch flags were used.

`Finding:` **equality over the corpus is only as stable as the corpus.** With one
intermittently nondeterministic scene in it, a single green comparison is weaker
evidence than it reads — and every earlier stage gate on this branch was a single
run. Recorded against future stage gates.
