2.The paths, and their tradeoffs

Three live candidates, not mutually exclusive. The real question was ordering, and the owner answered it.

Decided

Films first, from the test-case portfolio (path B)

Decision
Decided 2026-08-04 by the owner: path B, diverse scenes from the test-case portfolio, first. REP4 follows. Path C's document amendment lands whenever convenient.
Why
Everything in section 1. A film converts eleven days of meta investment into observed value, feeds REP4's design with a fresh motivating instance, and tests the docs cold. The candidates are a Phase-3/4 portfolio film (the-briefing, rube-goldberg) or a small commission-register short.
Against
The extent-defect class that REP4 exists for is still unguarded, so the film will likely hit it. Hitting it under instruments that can measure it (probe) is also exactly the motivating evidence REP4's design wants fresh. The counter-argument, ship the guard before the film that needs it, is real, which is why the brainstorm stated a lean and left the call to the owner.
Implication
REP5's film starvation resolves by the same decision, and the flywheel gets something to grind.
Near-term
The router's work-next row carries the queue: market-crash, boss-intro, and the 2D-explainer rung as the in-reach picks. Each film ends with the method's film field report.

Evidence: plan.md's test-case portfolio; postmortem 2026-08-03, forward item 6 (the deferred docs-only test); docs/README.md's work-next row.
